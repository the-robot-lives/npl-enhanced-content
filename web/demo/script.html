start-script

A <sem-shell> with a start-script attribute does not play at boot: it starts the first time it is half in view, pauses when it leaves view or the tab hides, resumes when it is looked at again, and plays once per page load. Escape stops it and hands the terminal back. The shell on this page starts below the fold — scroll down to watch it begin.
